Research

I am a biomedical engineer and neuroscience researcher with a background in computational modeling, AI-driven data analysis, and physiological signal processing. My current doctoral research focuses on using contrastive learning and deep learning representations to analyze clinical neural and behavioral data, aiming to uncover the underlying mechanisms of human motor control and its disorders.
